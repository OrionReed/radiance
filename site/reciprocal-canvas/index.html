reciprocal HRC canvas

A window onto a larger world that moves: rooms, lights and doors, and a bright source far to the west behind a fence. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom.

Reciprocal HRC solves a square around the view. Light from outside it, from boxes at any distance and from the sky, comes in along the lines that cross the square's edge: every frame, each line takes the light of the first thing it meets beyond the edge. Nothing outside is kept between frames.
