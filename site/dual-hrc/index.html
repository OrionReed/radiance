dual HRC

The dual runs HRC's computation backwards. In the dual extensions, light leaves each light; each cone hands a share to its own rays and carries the rest to the probes where those rays end. In dual gathering, the rays are halved back down to the c0 rays, which splat into the pixels they cross. Solid lights enter from their faces, each face's near field laid down from a table. The splats get one cross blur at the end, as the primal's output does. The output is fluence averaged over each pixel; the primal's is at the pixel's probe. Mono.
