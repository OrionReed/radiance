dual column form

Reciprocal HRC with exact counts. The atoms are dual pixels: the right half of one column and the left half of the next, centred on the plane between them. A segment of L atoms has rises 0 … L − 1 in one octant (steps 0 or +1); each frustum runs two octants, its rows as they are and mirrored.

Every level is one image per octant: column block·L + s. Up, a parent (2L, s) is its first half (L, a), a step of j rows at the pixel centre between them, and its second half (L, b), a + j + b = s. Three such paths are mixed by the linear rule, so the mean junction row is the centre line's. Down runs the same terms backwards: a second half takes its share of the parent's light through the first half, plus the first half's light times the Crofton line measure of the parent. The last down step gives each atom's light, and a pixel is the mean of its left edge and its centre.

Wu: where a parent has three terms, its light leans toward the term with the higher junction row by ¼ of its slope across rows.
